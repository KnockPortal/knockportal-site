Where this comes from

Every point is a public record. San Francisco publishes building permits as open data; we pull them several times a day, place each one on its parcel, and look for permits that land close together in space and time.

You can check any address on this map against the city's own record — the links in the popups go straight there.

Why this isn't a lead list

A permit means that job is sold. The orange points are your competitors' work, not your prospects.

What's worth your morning is the blue: the houses on those same blocks that haven't been re-roofed in years, whose owners have just spent three weeks watching scaffolding go up next door.

We watch the source, too

On July 1 the city quietly moved roofing permits to a new system. Anyone reading the old feed saw the market drop 91% — it hadn't. July was the strongest month of the year.

Both datasets are public and free. If you pull San Francisco permits yourself, count them by month per category and look for the cliff — ours is at July 1.
